PLAYER SERVICE NOTICE

READ BEFORE PLACING AN ORDER

SKIN GIFTING PROCESS

Please review the process below before continuing. Requirements and delivery timing may vary slightly depending on the selected game.

01

Gifter & Delivery Details

After your order is placed, the Gifter details and estimated Delivery Date (DD) will normally be provided within 1–24 hours. We will send updates through our official page or your order information.

02

Processing Status

When your order status changes to PROCESSING, your order has already been submitted to the assigned sender. Refresh your order page periodically until the sender's Gifter ID / IGN and Delivery Date appear in the order message.

03

Follow the Assigned Gifter

Follow or add the assigned Gifter as soon as the details are provided. Some games require an in-game friendship period before gifting becomes available. For example, Mobile Legends: Bang Bang currently requires an 8-day in-game friendship period. Do not remove or unfollow the Gifter while your order is active, as this may delay delivery.

04

Check Your Order Updates

To view the latest information, open your account menu, go to Processing, select the order, then open the INV# to view the complete order details and any new messages.

Important: Please do not contact the assigned Gifter for personal or unrelated reasons. Doing so may result in the order being delayed or cancelled.

DELIVERY WINDOW

Skin gifting is generally processed between 9:00 PM and 12:00 midnight. A delay of approximately one additional day may occasionally occur because of game-server resets, gifting limits, maintenance, or other publisher-side restrictions outside our control.

NEED HELP?

For assistance, please contact GAMERXTREAM through our official page and provide your order or invoice number so we can assist you faster.

By selecting ACCEPT & CONTINUE, you confirm that you have read and understood the Skin Gifting process and agree to follow the instructions above.
IMPORTANT INFORMATION

Notice for MLBB Products

Refundable Transactions — Diamonds and Double Diamonds

Transactions for Diamonds and Double Diamonds are eligible for a refund when the order returns any of the following messages:

  • Region Lock
  • Order Not Found
  • Failed
  • Purchase Limit
  • Inquiry Error
  • Invalid ID

Refundable Transactions — Weekly Diamond Pass

Transactions for the Weekly Diamond Pass are eligible for a refund only when the order returns one of the following messages:

  • Region Lock
  • Invalid ID
  • Order Not Found
  • Inquiry Error
Important: Weekly Diamond Pass transactions showing Purchase Limit or Failed are not refundable.

Refund Processing

For the refundable error messages listed above, there is no need to contact Surae Digital Shop Customer Service. Eligible refunds will be processed automatically within 1–12 hours.
